Regulatory review,
with the receipts.

CLARITY retrieves the public evidence for a clinical programme, organises it into a cited nine-domain dossier, and scores it the way an FDA reviewer would. Every score, every finding and every probability traces back to the source document — and to the exact retrieval command that found it.
